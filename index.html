      How output works

      1. Record always works — program + mixer audio are written to a WebM file on your machine.
      2. Stream publishes program over WebRTC to a WHIP endpoint you control.
      3. Your relay re-encodes and pushes RTMP to any platform (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick…).

      Nothing leaves your browser until you press Stream.
